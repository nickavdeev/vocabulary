Privacy Policy for Vocabulary Bot

Last updated: 25.10.2025

1. Developer Information

This bot is developed and maintained by an individual developer based in Germany.
Contact: vocabulary@avdeev.me

2. Summary of the Bot’s Functionality

Vocabulary Bot (https://t.me/VocabMateBot) helps users look up word definitions, save them to a personal vocabulary list, and receive spaced reminders to review them based on the forgetting curve.

3. What Data We Collect

When you use the bot, we collect the following data:

  • Telegram user ID
  • Language settings (as provided by Telegram)
  • Messages you send to the bot
  • Words you add to your vocabulary
  • Timestamps of your activity
  • All messages and interactions are logged along with your Telegram ID

This data is stored securely in our own database to provide core functionality such as spaced reminders and personalized vocabulary lists.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Your consent, which you give by using the bot
  • Our legitimate interest in providing and improving the service

5. Data Usage

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Display and manage your personal vocabulary
  • Send reminders based on your learning schedule
  • Improve the functionality of the bot

We do not sell, monetize, or share your data with third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the bot’s functionality. You can request deletion of all your data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures to protect your data and prevent unauthorized access or disclosure. All data is stored securely and handled in accordance with GDPR.

8. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the data we store about you
  • Correct or update your data
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Object to or restrict processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us at vocabulary@avdeev.me. We respond within 30 days.

9. Consent

By using Vocabulary Bot, you consent to this Privacy Policy and the processing of your data as described above.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this publicly accessible page.
